## 1. What this app is

Bequest is a single-player, text-based life simulator for Android. You play
one life a year at a time, then play the life of whoever inherits.

**There is no Bequest server.** No account, no login, no profile. Nothing is
uploaded to us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

## 2. What is stored, and where

Everything lives in the app's own storage on your phone. Nothing here is
sent to us.

| Key | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `bequest.save`, `bequest.slot1-3` | Your saved lives: the character, family, money, career, history |
| `bequest.meta` | Progress across lives: Legacy Points, achievements, records, unlocks, whether Plus is active |
| `bequest.cloud` | Your cloud sync settings, if you turned it on: the code and the server address you typed |
| `bequest.crashlog` | Up to twelve problem reports, described in section 5 |
| `bequest.lastslot`, `bequest.migrated` | Housekeeping |

The names in your saves are generated by the game from name lists. They are
not yours and we never ask for yours.

## 3. Cloud sync is optional, and the server is one you choose

Bequest has an optional sync feature. **It does not sync to us.** You type a
server address and a sync code, and the app sends your saves to that
address. We do not run it, cannot see it, and cannot recover anything from
it.

If you turn this on:

- Your saves leave your device and go to **the operator of the address you
  typed**. Their privacy practices apply, not ours. Only name a server you
  trust.
- Anyone who knows your sync code can read whatever is stored under it on
  that server. Treat the code as a password.
- Saves coming back are checked for shape before they are written, and
  Bequest Plus is never restored from a sync. It belongs to your purchase.

Leave sync off and nothing ever leaves the phone.

## 4. What we do not collect

- No name, email, phone number or address. We never ask.
- No advertising identifier. No ad networks. No third-party SDKs.
- No analytics. No usage tracking. No session recording.
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
- No payment details. See section 6.

## 5. Problem reports

If the game hits an error it writes a short report to your phone so you can
tell us what happened. **These are not sent anywhere.** There is no
destination in this version. The switch that would allow sending is off by
default and currently says so.

A report contains the shape of the life and nothing about a person:

- Included: app version, error message and technical trace, your age in
  game, generation, country setting, difficulty, which screen you were on,
  the last few actions you took, current medical conditions in game, and
  your in-game money as a **band** such as `10k-100k`.
- **Never included:** your character's name, any other character's name,
  employer, school or manager names, any line from your life story, or your
  save file.

You can read the reports in the app, copy one to send it on yourself, or
delete all of them, under More, Problem reports.

## 6. Payments and ads

In this version the Bequest Plus purchase screens and the optional ad for a
Second Chance are **simulated**. No payment is taken, no card details are
handled, and no ad network is contacted. When real billing is added this
policy will be rewritten before the version ships.

## 7. Children

Bequest contains crime, alcohol, drugs and gambling as life events. It is
not directed at children and is not enrolled in the Play Families
programme. Expect a 16+ or Mature rating.

## 8. Your control over the data

- **Delete a life:** delete the save slot in game.
- **Delete problem reports:** More, Problem reports, Delete the reports.
- **Delete everything:** clear the app's storage in Android settings, or
  uninstall. Nothing survives elsewhere, because we hold nothing.
- **Export:** the game can export a save as a file you keep.

There is no account to close and no deletion request to send us, because we
have nothing of yours to delete.

## 9. Backups

Android may include app data in the operating system backup, under Google's
rules rather than ours. Turn off backup for Bequest in Android settings if
you do not want that.
