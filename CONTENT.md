# BEQUEST — Content Specification
**Every activity, decision and popup in the game.**
v1.0 · grounded tone · global setting · birth to death · USD

---

## 0. Popup taxonomy

Every interruption in the game is one of six types. Nothing appears on screen that isn't one of these.

| Type | Trigger | Player input | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Event Popup** | Fires on AGE | 2–4 choices | "A bigger kid demands your lunch money." |
| **B. Notification** | Fires on AGE | Acknowledge only | "You turned 13. Your body is changing." |
| **C. Action Result** | After an activity | Acknowledge only | "You worked out. Fitness +3, Health +2." |
| **D. Confirmation** | Before a costly/risky action | Confirm / Cancel | "Quit your job as Junior Analyst?" |
| **E. Roll Result** | After a risk action | Acknowledge only | "You were caught. Arrested." |
| **F. Screen** | Menu navigation | Full page | Activities, Relationships, Assets, Summary |

**Yearly popup order on AGE:**
`Birthday notification → habit ticks → bills/salary → health/aging check → world news → 0–3 events → death check`

---

## 1. Screens (Type F)

1. **Title** — New Life · Continue · Challenges · Settings · About
2. **Character Creation** — name, gender, country, then Random Birth or Custom Start (Custom = premium)
3. **Main HUD** — age, name, job, cash, net worth · 6 stat bars · life log feed · AGE button · bottom nav
4. **Activities** — grid of actions, filtered by life stage
5. **Relationships** — list of NPCs with relationship bars
6. **NPC Detail** — their stats, history with you, interaction list
7. **Skills** — 8 skills with unlock tiers shown
8. **Habits** — 7 habits with addiction levels and yearly effects
9. **Career** — current job, ladder position, promotion requirements, job listings
10. **Assets** — cash, savings, investments, property, vehicles, inventory
11. **Shop** — items by category with stated effects
12. **News** — world events feed and current economic modifiers
13. **Challenges** — cross-run goals and Legacy Points
14. **Death Summary** — recap, epitaphs, family tree, continue-as options

## 2. Stats, Skills, Habits

**Stats (0–100):** Health · Happiness · Smarts · Looks · Reputation · Discipline

**Skills (0–100), with unlock tiers:**

| Skill | 25 | 50 | 75 | 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cooking | Cook at home (save money) | Line cook job | Chef job | Restaurant owner |
| Writing | Blog | Freelance writing | Journalist | Novelist / bestseller events |
| Gaming | Casual streams | Small streamer income | Esports tryout | Pro esports contract |
| Handiness | DIY repairs | Handyman gigs | Contractor | Construction firm |
| Fitness | Better health decay | Gym trainer job | Athlete tryout | Pro athlete |
| Charisma | Better social rolls | Sales jobs | Management track | Politics / executive |
| Business | Understand investments | Manage a business | Multi-business | Corporate empire |
| Combat | Win fights | Security job | Military/police advancement | Crime boss / champion |

**Habits (addiction 0–100). Yearly tick shown in-game:**

| Habit | Cost/yr | Yearly effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smoking | $2,400 | Health −3, Looks −1 | Cancer risk scales with years |
| Drinking | $1,800 | Health −2, Happiness +1 | Liver events, DUI risk |
| Junk food | $1,500 | Health −2, Looks −2, Happiness +1 | Obesity, diabetes chain |
| Caffeine | $900 | Discipline +1, Health −1, Sleep −1 | Mild; anxiety at high levels |
| Gym | $600 | Health +3, Looks +2, Fitness +2 | Positive habit |
| Good sleep | $0 | Health +2, Smarts +1, Discipline +1 | Positive habit |
| Gambling | varies | Money swing, Happiness volatile | Debt spiral chain |

Quitting = multi-year struggle with yearly relapse rolls weighted by Discipline.

## 3. ACTIVITIES — the full list

Gated by life stage. Each shows its cost, time and effects before you commit.

### Always available (all ages)
| Activity | Effects |
|---|---|
| Doctor visit | Health +, costs money, may reveal illness |
| Rest / take it easy | Health +, Happiness + |
| Walk / spend time outdoors | Health +1, Happiness +2, free |

## 4. DECISIONS — the branching choice points

### Life-stage forks (always fire)
| Age | Decision |
|---|---|
| 6 | Which school type (public / private / homeschool) |
| 13 | Which crowd do you fall in with (nerds / athletes / troublemakers / loners) |
| 16 | First job or focus on school |
| 18 | College · trade school · work · military · gap year · crime |
| 22 | First career path |
| ~25–35 | Marriage / children / stay single |
| ~30–50 | Buy vs rent · career risk vs stability |
| 65 | Retire or keep working |
| Any | Emigrate to another country |

### Recurring dilemmas
- **Money vs happiness** — the high-paying job you hate
- **Risk vs safety** — startup vs salary; crypto vs savings
- **Health vs pleasure** — habits, every single year
- **Loyalty vs opportunity** — cheat, betray a friend, snitch, leave family behind
- **Honesty vs gain** — lie on a CV, tax fraud, plagiarise
- **Self vs family** — care for a sick parent vs your career

Every decision writes a **flag** that later events read. Nothing is forgotten.
